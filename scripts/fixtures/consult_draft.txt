Emily Lee is a 54 female. She initially palpated a mass in the medial aspect of the left breast. She underwent follow up imaging which confirmed a suspicious lesion in the left breast. She had a core needle biopsy of the abnormality in the left breast in April 2025. This showed invasive ductal carcinoma, grade 2, ER positive 8/8, PR positive 4/8, HER2 equivocal via IHC and negative via fish.

On May 15, 2025, she had a left partial mastectomy and sentinel lymph node procedure with Dr. Anderson. The pathology showed a 3 cm invasive ductal carcinoma, grade 2, with evidence of lymphovascular invasion. Margins were clear. 1 of 4 sentinel lymph nodes involved, measuring 2.1 mm. There was no extranodal extension. pT2N1.

She had an Oncotype DX test done in August 2025 which showed a recurrence score of 20. This corresponds with a distant recurrence risk at 9 years of 5% with hormonal therapy alone. There is no chemotherapy benefit.

I will recommend 5 years of adjuvant letrozole. She will need a bone mineral density test. I will need to refer her to Radiation Oncology for consideration of adjuvant radiation. I will not recommend adjuvant chemotherapy. I will assess her again in 3 months.
